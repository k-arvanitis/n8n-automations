# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from buyers, especially those purchasing from abroad.

## Can foreigners buy property in Greece?

Yes. There are no restrictions on EU or non-EU citizens buying property in most of Greece. A small number of border and island regions require a simple permit for non-EU buyers, but this does not affect Athens or the areas we cover.

## What is the Golden Visa threshold?

The Golden Visa grants a five-year renewable residence permit to non-EU buyers who invest in Greek property. As of the current rules, the minimum investment is 800,000 EUR in high-demand areas including Athens, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, and Santorini, and 400,000 EUR in the rest of the country. The thresholds have risen over time, so we confirm the figure that applies to a specific property before you commit.

## Can non-residents get a mortgage in Greece?

Yes, though it is more limited than for residents. Greek banks typically lend non-residents up to 60 to 70 percent of the property value, subject to proof of income and a Greek bank account. Many foreign buyers purchase in cash or finance from their home country, as approval can take several weeks.

## What documents do I need to buy?

At minimum: a valid passport or ID, a Greek tax number (AFM), and proof of funds. For a mortgage you will also need income documentation and tax returns. Your lawyer will give you a complete checklist tailored to your situation.

## How long does the purchase take?

A typical purchase takes 8 to 12 weeks from accepted offer to registration. Clean cash purchases can complete in around 6 weeks; mortgage purchases or those with title complications take longer.

## Are property prices negotiable?

Often, yes. Asking prices usually leave some room, though the margin depends on the property, how long it has been listed, and current demand. We advise you on a realistic offer and negotiate on your behalf.

## Do I need to be in Greece to complete the purchase?

No. You can grant a limited power of attorney to your lawyer, who can then sign on your behalf. Many of our foreign clients complete the entire process remotely.

## What rental yield can I expect?

Gross rental yields in our areas typically range from about 3.5 percent in prime central locations to 5.5 to 6 percent in higher-yield areas such as Piraeus and for well-run short-term rentals. Net yield depends on management costs, taxes, and occupancy.

## Do you offer property management?

Yes. We offer full property management for owners who rent out their property, covering tenant sourcing, rent collection, maintenance coordination, short-let listing management, and bill payment. Fees are agreed per property.

## What are the ongoing costs of owning a property?

The main recurring costs are the annual ENFIA property tax, building shared charges (koinochrista) for apartments, utilities, and any management fees. We can estimate these for any listing.

## Is now a good time to buy in Athens?

Athens property has seen sustained price growth driven by tourism, the Golden Visa programme, and limited new supply, though growth rates vary by area. We give every client an honest, data-based view rather than a sales pitch.

## Can I buy a property to use for short-term rental (Airbnb)?

Yes, short-term letting is legal and popular in Athens, subject to registration with the tax authorities and any building regulations. Central and tourist-friendly areas such as Pangrati, Kolonaki, and Piraeus perform well. Note that some buildings restrict short-term lets in their bylaws, which we always check.

## What is the objective value and why does it matter?

The objective value is the property value the state assigns for tax purposes, often lower than the market price. Transfer tax and ENFIA are calculated on it, so it directly affects your costs. We can look up the objective value for any property.

## Do you help with opening a Greek bank account?

Yes. A Greek bank account is needed to pay taxes, utilities, and (for a mortgage) the loan. We guide you through the requirements and can recommend banks that are accustomed to working with foreign buyers.

## What happens after I buy? Do you stay involved?

We can stay involved as much as you wish, from a one-off handover to full ongoing property management. Many clients use us to set up utilities, find tenants, and handle the property while they are abroad.
