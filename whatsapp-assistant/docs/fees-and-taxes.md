# Fees and Taxes When Buying in Greece

Below are the typical costs on top of the purchase price. As a rough guide, budget an extra 8 to 10 percent of the property price to cover all fees and taxes on a standard resale purchase.

## Property Transfer Tax

The property transfer tax is 3.09 percent of the property's taxable value (the higher of the contract price and the objective tax value set by the state). This tax is paid by the buyer and must be settled before the final contract is signed. Newly built properties sold by a developer with a building permit issued after 2006 may instead be subject to VAT, though this VAT has been suspended for new builds under current rules; we confirm the correct treatment for each property.

## Notary Fees

Notary fees are regulated and run at approximately 1.2 percent of the property value, plus VAT on the fee and minor charges for copies and certificates. The notary is mandatory and drafts the official transfer contract.

## Agency Commission

Our agency commission is 2 percent of the purchase price, plus 24 percent VAT on that commission. The commission is payable on successful completion of the purchase. We agree this in writing before you make an offer, so there are no surprises.

## Legal Fees

Lawyer fees are typically 1 to 1.5 percent of the property value for a standard transaction, sometimes with a minimum fee for lower-priced properties. The lawyer handles due diligence, the title search, and registration.

## Land Registry and Cadastre Registration Fees

Registration fees at the Land Registry and Cadastre come to roughly 0.5 to 0.7 percent of the property value, covering the official recording of the transfer that makes you the legal owner.

## Annual Property Tax (ENFIA)

ENFIA is the annual property ownership tax, payable every year by the owner. The amount depends on the property's size, location, age, and floor, calculated on the objective tax value rather than the market price. For a typical apartment it ranges from a few hundred to a couple of thousand euros per year. We can provide an ENFIA estimate for any specific listing on request.

## Worked Example

For a 300,000 EUR resale apartment, approximate one-off costs are:

- Transfer tax (3.09 percent): about 9,270 EUR
- Notary fee (1.2 percent plus VAT): about 4,460 EUR
- Agency commission (2 percent plus VAT): about 7,440 EUR
- Legal fee (about 1.2 percent): about 3,600 EUR
- Registration (about 0.6 percent): about 1,800 EUR

Total additional costs: roughly 26,500 EUR, or about 8.8 percent on top of the price. Exact figures depend on the property's objective value and the professionals you engage.
