# Athens Neighbourhood Guide

Profiles of the areas where Arion Real Estate is most active. Average prices are indicative for resale apartments in good condition and change with the market.

## Kolonaki

- Average price: 5,500 to 7,500 EUR per sqm
- Character: Athens' most prestigious central district, known for designer boutiques, art galleries, and embassies. Elegant pre-war and neoclassical buildings.
- Transport: Walking distance to Evangelismos metro (Line 3); central and walkable.
- Amenities: High-end shopping on Skoufa and Tsakalof, cafes around Kolonaki Square, museums (Benaki, Goulandris), Lycabettus Hill for green space.

Best for buyers who want a prestige central address and strong long-term value retention.

## Glyfada

- Average price: 4,000 to 6,000 EUR per sqm
- Character: The leading southern coastal suburb, often called the Athenian Riviera. A mix of villas, modern apartments, and a lively shopping and dining scene.
- Transport: Coastal tram to the centre; about 20 minutes to the airport by car.
- Amenities: Beaches and marinas, the Glyfada golf course, large shopping streets, international restaurants, and good private schools nearby.

Best for families, holiday-home buyers, and Golden Visa investors who want sea access and a resort lifestyle.

## Kifisia

- Average price: 3,500 to 5,500 EUR per sqm
- Character: A green, upscale northern suburb with mansions, leafy streets, and a cool summer microclimate. Kefalari is its most exclusive pocket.
- Transport: Northern terminus of metro Line 1; suburban rail links.
- Amenities: International schools, the Kifisia shopping district, parks, and high-end dining.

Best for families relocating with children and buyers who want space and greenery while staying connected to the city.

## Piraeus

- Average price: 2,200 to 3,500 EUR per sqm
- Character: Greece's largest port city, increasingly popular for regeneration and investment. Kastella and Mikrolimano offer attractive sea-view living.
- Transport: Metro Lines 1 and 3, the main port for the islands, suburban rail.
- Amenities: Marinas, seafront tavernas, the new port developments, and growing cultural venues.

Best for investors chasing higher rental yields and buyers who want sea views at a lower entry price than the southern suburbs.

## Pangrati

- Average price: 3,000 to 4,500 EUR per sqm
- Character: A central, characterful neighbourhood that has become one of the most desirable areas for younger professionals and creatives. Vibrant but residential.
- Transport: Walkable to the centre; close to Evangelismos metro; new metro Line 4 stations planned nearby.
- Amenities: Plastira Square cafes, the Panathenaic Stadium, the National Garden, independent restaurants and bars.

Best for buyers who want central city life with neighbourhood character, and for short-let investors.

## Marousi

- Average price: 2,800 to 4,000 EUR per sqm
- Character: A major northern commercial and residential hub, home to corporate headquarters and the former Olympic complex. A balance of business and family living.
- Transport: Metro Line 1, suburban rail, and quick access to the Attiki Odos motorway.
- Amenities: The Mall Athens, OAKA sports complex, offices, and good schools.

Best for commercial buyers, professionals working in the northern business district, and families wanting good transport links.
